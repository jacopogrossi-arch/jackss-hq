## Sfondi astratti — moodboard, techboard, cartelle, costruzione idea

Testati il 15/09/2026 su ChatGPT, tutti confermati ok. A differenza del prompt sopra (fotografico/editoriale), questi sono sfondi **non figurativi/artistici**, pensati per reggere sotto contenuti (collage, campioni colore, materiali, schemi) senza competere con loro. Fil rouge comune: azzurro sempre presente come accento, base neutra bianco/sabbia. Tecnica assegnata per funzione della tavola, non a caso.

**Figurini e disegni tecnici fronte/retro delle 5 giacche non sono in questa lista**: si fanno più avanti, uno sfondo diverso per ciascuna giacca che rispetti il suo mood specifico, una volta pronto l'outfit — non uno sfondo generico riciclato su tutte e 5.

### Moodboard evocativa (acquerello astratto)

> Abstract watercolor background, non-figurative, no recognizable objects. Soft overlapping washes of azure blue and warm off-white/sand tones bleeding into each other, loose organic edges, visible paper grain, light desaturated pigment pooling. Airy, emotional, evocative mood — feels like a page from a designer's travel sketchbook, not a photograph or illustration. Muted, low-contrast, plenty of open pale areas for a moodboard collage to be pasted on top. A3 landscape proportions (420x297mm), high resolution, no text, no logos, no photographic elements, no figures.

### Moodboard tecnica (linee di movimento, più strutturate)

> Abstract technical background, non-figurative. Fine sketched lines suggesting motion and direction — swept, gestural strokes like wind or velocity trails — in azure blue ink on a warm off-white/sand paper texture, arranged with a structured, almost blueprint-like rhythm rather than painterly chaos. Subtle grid or graphite pencil texture underneath. Restrained, technical mood, not decorative. Low visual noise, most of the surface left calm and open for technical annotations to be added on top. A3 landscape proportions (420x297mm), no text, no logos, no photographic elements, no figures.

### Cartella cromatica (quasi vuoto, velo minimo)

> Extremely minimal abstract background, almost a plain surface. Warm off-white/sand paper or canvas texture, visible but very subtle grain, with only a faint hint of pale azure blue bleeding softly along one edge of the frame — the rest completely calm and neutral. No strong color, no brushstrokes, no pattern, no objects, no text. The surface should read as nearly blank so color swatches can be placed on top without visual competition. A3 landscape proportions (420x297mm), high resolution, subtle print-paper texture.

### Cartella materiali (texture materiale astratta)

> Abstract macro material texture, non-figurative, not identifiable as any specific real object. A tactile, close-up abstract surface blending raw linen weave texture with soft mineral/salt-crystal graininess, in warm off-white/sand tones with a faint azure undertone bleeding through in places. Physical, textile-like, slightly rough — evokes touch rather than image. Muted, low contrast, no sharp focal point, so material swatches can be placed on top. A3 landscape proportions (420x297mm), no text, no logos, no recognizable objects, no figures.

### Costruzione idea (linee di movimento, più marcate/dinamiche)

> Abstract gestural background, non-figurative. Bold sweeping azure blue ink lines and trails suggesting speed, wind and mechanical motion, layered with looser watercolor-like bleeds in warm off-white/sand — more dynamic and energetic than the moodboard background, carrying visible movement and directional energy across the page. Painterly but controlled, editorial, not chaotic. Enough calm negative space left for diagrams and construction notes on top. A3 landscape proportions (420x297mm), no text, no logos, no photographic elements, no figures.
