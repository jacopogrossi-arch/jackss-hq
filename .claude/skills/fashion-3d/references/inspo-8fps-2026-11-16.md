# What the reference reels do, studied at 8 fps (16/11/2026)

Sheets of 24 frames = 3 s, read left to right. Source: `iCloud › INSPO 3D`. Instagram watermark and UI excluded.

## @holcdenis, "No Blender. Just Claude" (21 s, a diorama inside a monitor)
- The CAMERA is almost fixed; the choreography of objects does the work. Something changes every 1-1.5 s:
  - buildings rise in staggered waves, 1-2 frames apart (the city builds in ~1 s);
  - a "scanner" character acts on every card (red laser line);
  - cards swap with a 3-frame sideways slide with real motion blur;
  - the weather/time change is a continuous light crossfade of ~10 frames, windows lighting one by one;
  - "zero gravity": buildings detach and rise with a slow rotation.
- A command card names each change; the world reacts to it. Closing ask is a card inside the scene ("COMMENT SKILL").

## drillup.io, claw machine / Monopoly board / gym (metaphor worlds)
- An abstract idea becomes a physical game: a claw machine = one more try costs nothing; a Monopoly board = money between friends; a gym = reps.
- The camera NEVER stops: a slow continuous glide with very shallow depth of field (miniature feel), following the object that acts (a card flies in an arc with rotation, lands with a shadow, the camera follows to the next piece).
- Every action starts ON its word ("so you *send*" = the card flies on "send"); captions word by word at one fixed band.
- Data are objects: a dot grid where "1 in 10" lights up in yellow; counters; a cube mascot that walks and reacts.
- Different camera idea per story beat: front of the machine, then sideways and down to the prize chute while the mascot walks.

## Our Shorts at 8 fps (same method)
- Short #1 (atelier): a static hold of 12 frames (1.5 s) where only the caption changes; few big whips of 3 frames.
- Short #2 (studio): the garment lies flat as one flat colour (no folds, no fabric sheen) while the caption changes for 1.5 s.
- Fix: never still > 0.8 s, continuous camera, reacting character, real fabric on the hero.
